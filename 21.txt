Brian Tracy outlines 21 fundamental principles for effective time management and success. Here they are as presented in the seminar:

1.  **Goals:** Define clear, written goals (2:21).
2.  **Organized Plans:** Create detailed action steps for your goals (5:04).
3.  **Analysis:** Always work from a written list (6:57).
4.  **Priorities:** Apply the 80/20 rule to focus on high-payoff tasks (8:42).
5.  **Concentration:** Focus single-mindedly on one task at a time (11:22).
6.  **Deadlines & Rewards:** Use deadlines as a forcing system (14:46).
7.  **Time Log:** Track where your time actually goes (16:59).
8.  **Overcoming Procrastination:** Develop a sense of urgency and use the 'do it now' mantra (18:54).
9.  **Delegation:** Outsource tasks that are not the best use of your time (21:25).
10. **Meetings:** Ensure every meeting has a clear purpose and agenda (23:37).
11. **Interruptions:** Control your environment to minimize distractions (25:47).
12. **Key Result Areas:** Know exactly why you are on the payroll (28:08).
13. **Batching Tasks:** Group similar tasks to benefit from the learning curve (30:42).
14. **Neatness:** Keep a tidy work environment (31:56).
15. **Chunks of Time:** Block out large segments of time for important work (34:59).
16. **Transition Time:** Use waiting or travel time to learn (37:26).
17. **Telephone:** Use the phone as a tool, not a social distraction (41:13).
18. **Punctuality/Attention:** Be punctual and pay close attention during conversations (46:22).
19. **Work Simplification:** Streamline processes to find faster ways to work (47:44).
20. **The Magic Word 'No':** Politely decline tasks that don't fit your goals (50:15).
21. **Balance:** Ensure your work habits support your quality of life (51:50).
